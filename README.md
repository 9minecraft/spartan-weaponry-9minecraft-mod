# Spartan Weaponry — Slay Enemies Using Medieval Weapons

Spartan Weaponry 9minecraft Mod adds a massive collection of medieval weapons to your game. You can craft everything from fast daggers to heavy greatswords using normal materials like wood, iron, and netherite. It gives you many fresh ways to fight mobs and survive in your world.

Lets you craft dozens of new melee weapons like katanas, rapiers, and battleaxes. Adds long polearms such as spears and halberds that give you extra reach to hit enemies from far away.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **ObliviousSpartan**, **xiyu**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Most of the porting work sits in the mixin layer. Each Minecraft release moves or renames the methods this mod hooks into, so the hooks are re-pointed and re-tested against that version rather than rewritten. The behaviour underneath is upstream's and is deliberately left alone — that is the part that makes the mod feel right, and it is not ours to redesign.
